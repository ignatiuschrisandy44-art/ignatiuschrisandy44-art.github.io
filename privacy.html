Privacy Policy for Tabungku

Effective date: 22 October 2026

Tabungku ("the app") is developed by Cresca Labs ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Your financial records stay on your device

Accounts, transactions, categories, exchange rates you enter, investments (with their terms, purchases, sales, income and prices), reminders and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not have a server, and we never receive, see, sell or share these records. Android's automatic cloud backup of the app is turned off. Your records only leave your phone when you choose to back them up as described below. If you uninstall the app without a backup, the records are deleted.

Backups to your Google Drive (Tabungku Pro)

If you use Settings › Backup & restore, the app copies your records (the same data as above, plus your main currency and fee settings) into a backup file in your own Google Drive, in the hidden application data folder that only Tabungku can open. This happens when you tap Back up now, and daily or weekly if you turn on automatic backup. To do this the app asks Google for permission to "see, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive"; it cannot see any other files in your Drive. The backup goes directly from your phone to Google over an encrypted connection. We never receive it. Google stores it under your Google account according to Google's terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app keeps the newest five backups and deletes older ones.

You can restore a backup on this or another phone signed in to the same Google account. You can delete backups in the app, turn off automatic backup at any time, and remove all of Tabungku's Drive data and its access in your Google account settings (Drive › Settings › Manage apps, and https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

You can also save a backup file to a place you choose (your phone, a memory card or another cloud app) and open such a file to restore it. Where that file goes is up to you. Backup files and CSV exports aren't password-protected, so keep them somewhere safe.

Market data and exchange rates

To show stock prices, stock lists and exchange rates, the app downloads public market data from Yahoo Finance. With Tabungku Pro it also downloads crypto prices from CoinGecko, the world gold price from gold-api.com and fund prices from Yahoo Finance for the investments you track that way. Only what is needed for the request is sent: stock and fund symbols (for example "BBCA.JK"), coin identifiers (for example "bitcoin"), the words you type when searching for a stock, fund or coin, currency codes (for example "USD") and the dates of past prices a report needs. Quantities, prices you paid, balances and all other records are never sent. As with any internet request, the provider can see your device's IP address. Downloaded prices, lists and rates are stored on your phone so they can be shown offline. Interest, coupons, yields and reports are calculated on your phone.

Reports

PDF reports are created on your phone and saved where you choose with Android's file picker. The app doesn't send them anywhere; what happens to a saved or shared report is up to you. Reports contain your financial records, so keep them somewhere safe.

Purchases (Tabungku Pro)

Tabungku Pro is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never receive your payment details. The app receives from Google Play only whether Pro was bought on your Google account (and a purchase token used to confirm it), and remembers that on your phone. See Google Play's terms and Google's privacy policy for how Google handles purchases.

Google sign-in

The app uses Google sign-in to identify you on this device. It receives your name and email address from Google and stores them only on your phone. We do not receive them. If you use Drive backups, the same Google account holds them, and the app shows its email address on the backup screen. You can sign out at any time in Settings.

Advertising

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how ads are viewed and tapped. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice in Settings › Ad privacy choices. Learn more about how Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. Tabungku Pro doesn't show ads.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows reminders you created. Reminders are scheduled on the device and work without internet.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Records on your phone: uninstall Tabungku, or clear its storage in your phone's app settings. Signing out alone keeps your records on the phone.
  • Google Drive backups (Tabungku Pro): delete them in Settings › Backup & restore, or remove all of Tabungku's hidden data in Google Drive (Settings › Manage apps › Tabungku › Options › Delete hidden app data).
  • Tabungku's access to your Google account: remove it at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

We don't keep a copy of your records, so there is nothing else for us to delete. If you have a question, email us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 18.

Delete your Tabungku data

Tabungku: Money Tracker, by Cresca, keeps your records on your phone. We don't run a server and never receive your records, so you delete them yourself, in a few steps:

  1. Records on your phone: uninstall Tabungku, or clear its storage in your phone's app settings. Signing out alone keeps your records on the phone.
  2. Google Drive backups (Tabungku Pro): in Tabungku, open Settings › Backup & restore and delete your backups. Or, in Google Drive on the web, open Settings › Manage apps, find Tabungku and choose Options › Delete hidden app data.
  3. Tabungku's access to your Google account: remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Tabungku Pro purchases are handled by Google Play and stay with your Google account.

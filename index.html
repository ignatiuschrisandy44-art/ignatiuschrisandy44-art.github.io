Tabungku: Money Tracker

A personal money tracker for Android. Record your accounts in any currency, money in and out, and transfers; get reminded when a bill is due; and save reports as PDF. Every record stays on your phone.

  • Accounts, cash flow and your own categories, in any currency
  • Transfer reminders that arrive on time, even offline
  • Reports by month, quarter, year or your own dates, saved as PDF; export to CSV
  • Tabungku Pro: investments (stocks, time deposits, bonds, mutual funds, gold, crypto), backup to your own Google Drive, no ads

Coming soon to Google Play.
